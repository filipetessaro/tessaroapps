How it works

No typing, no judgment — just 2 to 5 minutes of voice a day.

🎙️ You speak

Record how you're feeling, your own way, no script.

🧠 The AI understands

Transcription and analysis identify emotions and recurring themes in what you say.

💡 You get an insight

A reflection and a follow-up question to think about — never a diagnosis.

📈 A timeline forms

Iris remembers your last few days and shows emotional patterns over time.

🚨 Crisis support

Built-in risk screening that points you to emergency help when needed.

🔒 Private by default

Anonymous use from the very first moment. Delete a recording or all your data anytime.
