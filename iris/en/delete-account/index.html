Delete account — Íris

Language: PT · EN · ES

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Íris app and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Directly in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Íris and tap the gear icon on the home screen.
  2. Tap "Delete all my data".
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no need to wait for an email confirmation.

Option 2 — By email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to contato@tessaroapps.com with the subject "Delete account". We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password, if you created one) in the authentication system.
  • All of your voice recordings and check-ins.
  • All transcripts and emotional analyses (emotions, themes, insights, mood).
  • Your progress: streak, achievements.
  • Any insight you have shared in the Community.

What may be kept

  • Subscription records (payment history) required by Google Play/RevenueCat for tax and support purposes — we never have access to your card details.
  • Database backups, retained for up to 30 days before being permanently discarded.
