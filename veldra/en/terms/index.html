The game

Veldra ("the game") is developed by Filipe Tessaro. By playing, you agree to these terms.

It is a turn-based dungeon RPG with D&D-inspired rules, played entirely offline on your device.

It contains mild fantasy violence (combat against monsters) and is not directed at children under 13.

Your progress

The game is provided "as is", with no guarantee of continuous operation, freedom from defects, or permanent preservation of what is saved on your device.
