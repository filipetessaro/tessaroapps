§1

The short version

Veldra collects nothing. There is no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDK gathering data about you.

Everything the game keeps — your character, the world chronicle, your language and your motion preference — stays on your device and never leaves it.

§2

What is stored on your device

The character you are playing and their pack; the world chronicle (places discovered and finished, where the story stands, the names of those who died); your chosen language and animation preference.

This is written to the app's local storage. None of it is sent to us or to anybody else.

§3

What is NOT collected

Name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, photos, files, or anything from your address book.

The game uses no cookies and no trackers: it does not open web pages.

§4

Children

The game contains mild fantasy violence (combat against monsters, D&D style) and is not directed at children under 13. Since nothing is collected, there is no children's data to protect.

§5

How to delete your data

The title screen has an Erase everything button, which removes the character and the world chronicle from the device, permanently.

Uninstalling the app also removes everything. Since nothing is ever sent to a server, there is nothing to request the deletion of elsewhere — which is why Veldra has no account-deletion page.

§6

If this changes

An online leaderboard is already written in the game's code and is switched off: no part of the app uses it today. If it — or any other feature that sends data — is turned on, this page is updated before that version ships, saying exactly what starts being sent.

The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
