§1

The summary

Monster Terrarium ("Terrário de Monstros", the game) is developed by Filipe Tessaro. The game does not collect data about you. There is no account, login, ads, analytics, or third-party SDK gathering information.

Everything the game stores stays on your computer and is not sent to us.

§2

What stays on your computer

The saved game (coins, monsters in the terrarium and in the box, eggs, bestiary, colors found, and achievements) and window preferences (monitor and strip width).

On Windows, these files live in your user's application data folder (%APPDATA%\terrario-de-monstros).

§3

What is NOT collected

Name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, files on your computer, what appears on your screen, or any other personal information.

The game sits on top of your desktop, but it does not read or record what is behind it. It does not make connections to servers during play; the only exception is opening these pages in your browser when you click the links in Settings.

§4

Steam

In the version distributed via Steam, the purchase, achievements, and cloud save (if enabled) go through Steam, by Valve Corporation, and follow Valve's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment data or your Steam account data.

§5

Children

The game has no violence: it's about hatching, caring for, and collecting creatures. Since nothing is collected, there is no children's data to protect.

§6

How to delete your data

Delete the %APPDATA%\terrario-de-monstros folder or uninstall the game. If you use Steam cloud save, disable it in the game's Steam properties first.

Since nothing is sent to us, there is nothing to request deletion of elsewhere, which is why the game has no account-deletion page.

§7

If this changes

If any feature that sends data is ever added (an online leaderboard, for example), this page is updated before that version ships, stating exactly what starts being sent.

The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version.
