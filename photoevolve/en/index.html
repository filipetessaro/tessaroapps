What the app does

Hardware Score

Instant technical analysis from the photo's metadata — blur risk, noise, and lens sharpness.

Photographer Score (AI)

Composition, lighting, technical execution, and narrative evaluated by artificial intelligence, with feedback specific to the photo's genre.

Gamification

Levels, badges, and a photo streak with Pro protection — track your evolution as a photographer over time.

Compare and Share

Compare similar photos to pick the best one, and build a shareable portfolio with your scores.

Batch Review (Pro)

Quickly screen an entire photo shoot — up to 60 photos ranked by technical quality, at no cost until you decide what to save.

Multilingual

Available in Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
