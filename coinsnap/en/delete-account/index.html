Delete account — Coin Value Snap

Language: PT · EN · ES

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This page explains how to request the deletion of your account in the Coin Value Snap app and of the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contato@tessaroapps.com from the same address registered in the app, with the subject "Delete account".
  2. We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.
  3. You receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email and password) in the authentication system.
  • Your coin collection, including photos, name, country, year, estimated value, rarity and notes.
  • Your profile and gamification data: nickname, level, XP, badges, streak and friend connections.

What may be kept

  • Subscription records (payment history) required by Google Play/RevenueCat for tax and support purposes, retained under their own policies — we never have access to your card details.
  • Database backups, kept for up to 30 days before being permanently discarded.
