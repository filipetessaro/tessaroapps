Privacy Policy — InBloom

Last updated: July 31, 2026

InBloom ("the App") is an intimacy game app for couples, published by Tessaro Development. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it's handled. InBloom is intended only for users 18 years of age or older.

1. Age restriction

By using InBloom you confirm you are at least 18 years old. The App does not verify age with government ID — it relies on your confirmation at the age gate. If you are under 18, do not use this App.

2. Data we collect

Account identifier. When you first open the App, an anonymous account is created for your device via Supabase Authentication. This account has no email address, name, phone number, or other identifying information attached to it — it is a random identifier used solely to control access to paid content.

Purchases. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymous account identifier and your purchase/subscription status (active, expired, etc.) so the App can unlock paid content. We do not receive or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address) — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

Content you enter in the App. Partner names, a shared "safe word," interest selections, quiz answers, confessions, bucket-list entries, and game history are stored only on your device using local storage. This information is never uploaded to our servers or shared with us, unless described otherwise below.

Explicit game content. Once your subscription unlocks paid content, the App downloads text-based game content (from our database) relevant to the games you play. This is one-directional — we send content to the App; we do not receive your gameplay choices back.

Referral codes. If you choose to invite another couple, the App generates a short code linked to your anonymous account ID and stores it on our servers. If someone enters that code, we record that a redemption happened (linking two anonymous account IDs) so both accounts can receive the referral bonus. No names, contact info, or other identifying data is exchanged — only the anonymous account IDs already described above.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used in the App.
  • No advertising identifiers are collected.
  • No crash-reporting or third-party marketing tools have access to your data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers beyond the anonymous account ID described above.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Data retention & deletion

Content you enter (names, confessions, bucket lists, etc.) lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. To request deletion of your anonymous server-side account and unlock status, contact us at the email below.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Since almost all personal content stays on your device under your control, most of these rights are exercised simply by editing or deleting that content in the App, or uninstalling it. For anything stored on our servers (your anonymous account/unlock status), contact us to request access or deletion.

7. Children's privacy

InBloom is not directed at, and must not be used by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
