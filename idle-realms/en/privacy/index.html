§1

About this document

Idle Realms: Rise ("the game") is developed by Filipe Tessaro. This policy explains what data the game collects and how it is used.

§2

Data we collect

  • Anonymous session identifier — created automatically the first time you open the game (via Supabase Auth, anonymous mode), with no sign-up, email or password required. It does not identify you personally.
  • Player name (optional) — chosen freely by you on the Character Sheet, used only for display on the boss leaderboard, if you choose to submit a record.
  • Country (optional) — selected manually by you, never detected via GPS or device location. Used only to filter the leaderboard by country.
  • Boss records (optional) — your boss fight times, submitted only if you tap "Submit record to the world leaderboard".
  • In-app purchases (where available) — processed by the Apple App Store / Google Play and by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier to validate the purchase. We have no access to your card or payment details.
§3

Data we do not collect

  • We do not ask for an email, password or login of any kind.
  • We do not collect GPS location.
  • We use no ads and no advertising SDKs — the game has no ads, period.
  • We do not sell any data to third parties.
§4

Where the data lives

Your game progress (level, items, gold and so on) is stored locally on your device. Only the player name, country and boss records (all optional, and only if you choose to submit them) go to our database (Supabase), used exclusively for the world leaderboard.

§5

Children

The game contains mild fantasy violence (D&D-style monster combat) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

§6

Your rights

You can play 100% of the content without submitting any optional data. To request removal of a leaderboard record, see Delete data, or reach us at the email below.

§7

Changes to this policy

We may update this document as the game changes. The date at the top of the page always reflects the most recent version.
