What the app does

The app is an organisation and community tool. It is not an auction house, a shop, a broker or a professional appraisal service, and nothing in it constitutes investment advice on collectibles.

If something urgent slips past the in-app controls, write to contato@tessaroapps.com.

§7

Trades between users

Read this section before agreeing to any trade.

GarageRank brings collectors together, but takes no part in the trades. We do not process payment, do not hold funds in escrow, do not verify authenticity or condition, do not handle shipping and are not a party to the agreement. The negotiation happens between you and the other person, at your own risk.

In practice: if the piece never arrives, arrives different from what was agreed, or the deal goes sour, we have no way to reverse it — there is no transaction of ours to charge back. Arrange carefully, prefer people with a history in the app, be wary of pressure to hurry, and do not send money in advance to a stranger.

Fraud or bad faith in a trade arranged through the app: report it in the app or write to us. We may suspend the account involved, but that is a disciplinary measure, not compensation.

§8

AI identification

The app uses artificial intelligence (Claude models, by Anthropic) to try to identify models from a photo, including shelf photos with several pieces.

The result is an estimate and it can be wrong: model, series, year and variation. It is not a certificate of authenticity, it is not a market valuation and it is not evidence in a negotiation. Always check the packaging, the base of the piece and reference sources before buying, selling or trading based on what the app suggested.
