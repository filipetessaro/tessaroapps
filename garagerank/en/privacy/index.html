§1

About this document

This policy explains what data the GarageRank app collects, what it is for and what rights you have over it. GarageRank is a cataloguing and community app for collectors of diecast model cars.

By creating an account and using the app, you agree to what is described here.

§2

Data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

DataPurpose
Email and passwordCreate and access your account. The password is managed by Supabase Auth — we never have access to it in plain text.
Username and avatar colourShow your profile to you and to other users (leaderboard, feed).
Collection items (name, rarity, scanned barcode)Build your personal garage inside the app.
Photos you upload of your carsShow them on the item card and, if published, in the community feed.
Photo used in "Identify with AI"Sent to the AI provider (see §4) only at the moment of use, to suggest the car model.
Subscription statusKnow whether your account is Premium (managed by RevenueCat, see §4).
Wishlist and items offered for tradeShow what you are looking for and what you offer, and connect people with matching interests.
Who you follow, clubs you belong to and likesBuild your feed and the club pages.
Direct messages exchanged with other usersDeliver the conversation to both sides. They are not used for any other purpose.
Blocks and reportsEnforce the block and review reported content. A report stores who reported, the target and the reason.
Active-day streak, score and badgesCalculate leaderboards and achievements.
Device notification identifier (push token)Send app notifications — a new message, activity on your content. It identifies the device, not you; it disappears when the account is deleted.

We do not collect payment data directly — that is handled by the app store (Google Play / App Store) and by RevenueCat.

§3

How we use the data

  • Authenticate you and keep your session active across devices.
  • Sync your personal collection to the cloud.
  • Show photos you choose to publish in the feed, visible to other users of the app.
  • Calculate public leaderboards (collector and photographer) — aggregate counts only, never the individual list of your cars.
  • Process your Premium subscription, where applicable.
  • Deliver direct messages, run clubs and enforce blocks and reports.
  • Send notifications about activity on your account, if you allow it.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties.

§4

Who we share it with

We use the following service providers (data processors), each with only what its function requires:

  • Supabase database, authentication and photo storage.
  • RevenueCat Premium subscription management.
  • Anthropic processes the photo you submit when you use the "Identify with AI" feature (Premium). The image is used only to generate the answer and is not used to train models.
  • Google Play / App Store process the subscription payment.
§5

Storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers, protected by Row Level Security — by default each user can only read or change their own collection items. We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

§6

Your rights (LGPD)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), you may at any time:

  • Confirm what data we hold about you and access it.
  • Correct incomplete or outdated data.
  • Request deletion of your data (see §7).
  • Request portability of your data to another service.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email in §10.

§7

Account deletion

How to request it: send an email to contato@tessaroapps.com with the subject "Delete my GarageRank account", from the email registered in the app. We confirm the request within 30 days.

What is erased: login account, profile (username, avatar colour), the entire car collection, uploaded photos and likes given/received.

What may be retained: database backups may retain the data for up to a further 30 days after the deletion is confirmed, before being permanently erased. We retain no data beyond that backup window.

Partial deletion: you do not have to delete the whole account to erase specific data — any car, photo or like can be removed directly inside the app, at any time, with no email request needed.

§8

Children

GarageRank is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children in that age range.

§9

Changes to this policy

We may update this document as the app changes. The date at the top of the page always reflects the most recent version.
